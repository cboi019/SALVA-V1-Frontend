🛠️ Technical Troubleshooting

Why is my transaction failing?

Ensure you have enough ETH for gas fees. If you are depositing tokens (like USDT), you must first Approve the contract to use your tokens before the final deposit transaction.

I can't see my savings plan!

Make sure your wallet is connected to the Sepolia Testnet. If you are on Mainnet or another network, the dashboard will not be able to pull your data from the contract.

🛡️ Security Notice

SALVA is a non-custodial protocol. We cannot:

  • Reset or recover your wallet seed phrase.
  • Revert or cancel a transaction once it's on-chain.
  • Unlock your funds before the goal or date is reached.

Always double-check your goal parameters before confirming.
