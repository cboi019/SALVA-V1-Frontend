SALVA

Commit to Your Financial Goals

SALVA is a decentralized, non-custodial smart contract that helps you save towards your goals. Lock your tokens until you reach your target or a specific date.

🔒 Time-Based Savings

Lock your tokens until a specific date. Perfect for long-term goals.

🎯 Goal-Based Savings

Save towards a target amount. Unlock when you reach your goal.

🔐 Non-Custodial

You maintain full control. Your keys, your crypto, your future.

How It Works

1

Create a Plan

Choose between time-based or goal-based savings commitment.

2

Fund Your Goal

Deposit whitelisted ERC20 tokens into your savings plan.

3

Stay Committed

Your funds are locked until you reach your goal or maturity date.

4

Withdraw & Celebrate

Once mature, withdraw your savings and enjoy your achievement!
