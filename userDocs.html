The SALVA User Guide

Mastering financial discipline through decentralized technology.

💡 The SALVA Philosophy

Traditional savings accounts make it too easy to "dip into" your savings. SALVA is a Commitment Protocol. We believe true financial freedom comes from the discipline to keep your funds locked until your goals are met. By locking your funds in a smart contract, you protect your future self from current impulses.

Financial Discipline

Locking funds ensures money intended for long-term goals stays exactly where it belongs.

Zero Human Error

No bank employee can freeze your account. The code executes automatically and unbiasedly.

🔗 Decentralization & Your Safety

SALVA is Non-Custodial. This means you are the sole owner of your vault.

  • No Middleman: Only the Smart Contract—unbreakable code on the blockchain—holds your funds. Not even the developers can touch them.
  • Transparency: Every deposit is recorded on a public ledger. You can verify your balance on-chain 24/7.
The Vending Machine: A bank is like a shopkeeper who can say "no." SALVA is like a vending machine. If you meet the conditions (the date or target), the machine must release the funds.

📋 Smart Contract API

Plan Creation

  • createTimeBasedPlan(address _token, string _description, uint256 _savingsDuration)
  • createGoalBasedPlan(address _token, string _description, uint256 _targetAmount)

Funding & Deposits

  • fundTimeBasedPlan(address _token, uint256 _id, uint256 _amount)
  • fundGoalBasedPlan(address _token, uint256 _id, uint256 _amount)

Requires IERC20 approve() before execution.

Withdrawals

  • withdrawFromTBS(uint256 _id, uint256 _amount): Checks block.timestamp >= maturityTime.
  • withdrawFromGBS(uint256 _id, uint256 _amount): Checks currentAmount >= targetAmount.

🛡️ Core Error Handling

Ensure your frontend handles these specific reverts:

  • SalvaV1__COMMITMENT_NOT_MATURE: Attempting to withdraw early.
  • SalvaV1__NOT_ALLOWED_TOKEN: Using a token not on the whitelist.
  • SalvaV1__TOKEN_MISMATCH_FOR_PLAN: Token provided doesn't match the plan's setup.
